# Documentary Web Shots — 20 native C++ compositions

1920x1080, 30 fps, 90 frames, H.264. Every frame is rendered by Blend2D from C++ geometry and native text; ffmpeg only encodes.

## Shots

- `d01_court_docket_filed` — PACER-style docket page: sealed entry, row scan and FILED stamp (Evidence & courts)
- `d02_sec_edgar_redline` — SEC 10-K page with a material-weakness redline and exhibit tab (Evidence & courts)
- `d03_wikipedia_revision_diff` — Wikipedia page with revision history and a rising diff card (Archives & search)
- `d04_wayback_calendar` — Wayback Machine calendar scrub with selected snapshot ring (Archives & search)
- `d05_search_results_warrant` — Public-records search results, sealed hit and knowledge panel (Archives & search)
- `d06_factcheck_verdict` — Fact-check claim, verdict meter and FALSE stamp (Data & verification)
- `d07_live_blog_update` — Dark live blog with a new breaking update card sliding in (Live & broadcast)
- `d08_data_chart_annotation` — Data desk with a native line chart, endpoint pulse and annotation (Data & verification)
- `d09_satellite_before_after` — Split before/after field plate with moving scan divider and hotspots (Data & verification)
- `d10_leaked_memo_redaction` — Leaked memo: the redaction bar retracts to expose the finding (Evidence & courts)
- `d11_phone_alerts` — Lock-screen alert stack: the newest notification expands (Interface & field)
- `d12_youtube_transcript` — Watch page with a live transcript rail and moving selection (Live & broadcast)
- `d13_forum_thread` — Locked forum thread with climbing votes and a new reply (Interface & field)
- `d14_press_release_wire` — Newswire release with distribution count and scrolling ticker (Live & broadcast)
- `d15_budget_table` — Federal budget table with scanning rows and growing comparison bars (Data & verification)
- `d16_obituary_archive` — Newsprint obituary with a halftone portrait and archive stamp (Archives & search)
- `d17_pdf_annotations` — Annotated PDF viewer: highlighter sweep and a comment bubble (Evidence & courts)
- `d18_newsletter_inbox` — Mail inbox with a new issue sliding in over the reading pane (Interface & field)
- `d19_podcast_transcript` — Podcast transcript with a native waveform and moving playhead (Live & broadcast)
- `d20_map_route` — Dark route map: drawn flight path, waypoint pulses and distance (Interface & field)

Source: `tools/doc_web_shots/`.
