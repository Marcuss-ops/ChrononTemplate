# Fast Intro v1 pack contract

## Purpose

A reusable short opening composition. This is the first base for a pack that
can grow into richer intros; its structure includes several coordinated
elements beyond a title and subtitle.

## Composition and camera

- 1920×1080 at 30 fps, screen-space 2D, no camera move or external assets.
- Warm ivory ground, restrained title, neutral accent, short kicker,
  supporting subtitle, three editable topic chips, and a graphic rail with
  moving marker accents.
- All visible text is configurable through the native C++ spec.

## Motion and timing

- 72 frames (2.4 seconds). Rail draws quickly, text and chips reveal in a
  compact stagger, and the marker accents sweep to a settled end state.

## Must not appear

- No date timeline, footage, portraits, logos or unprovided brand assets.

## Preview acceptance

- The full opening reads clearly at 854×480, has visible motion across the
  opening, middle and ending frames, and keeps every element within frame.
