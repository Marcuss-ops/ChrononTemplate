# Social reference styles V1

## Scene contract

- **Subject:** Three editable social evidence cards reconstructed from the user supplied YouTube captures: two X posts and one YouTube comment.
- **Composition:** 16:9 dark field. X cards use a dark post surface with large white text, subdued metadata and the familiar interaction row. One X variant places a phrase on a bright red band. The YouTube comment uses a black card framed by a heavy red rectangle over a subdued blurred image field.
- **Motion and timing:** Static composition previews only, one frame each. Motion is deferred until the static designs are reviewed.
- **Layers:** Background, card, outline/highlight, avatar initials, identity, body copy, metadata, and action controls are separate native shape and text layers.
- **Must not appear:** YouTube player chrome, unrelated browser UI, pasted screenshot pixels, invented photographic identity assets, clipped text, or motion/effects.
- **Preview acceptance:** Three inspected 854×480 renders showing the three distinct treatments and readable typography.

The reference captures are used as visual guides only. The sample identity and copy below are illustrative reconstruction content; they are not attributed to the people in the captures.
