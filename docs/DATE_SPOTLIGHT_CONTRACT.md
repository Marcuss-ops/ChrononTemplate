# Date Spotlight scene contract

## Subject and assets

- One archival portrait crop from the user-provided YouTube screenshot.
- Exact lower-third copy from the reference: `2025` and `ALEX PASSED AWAY`.

## Composition

- 1920×1080, black-green near-black background.
- Wide, centered archival image in the upper half, with a rough dark edge retained from the source crop.
- Thin warm-white horizontal divider across the canvas.
- Centered year and muted amber headline below the divider with a restrained glow.
- Sparse film grain only; no interface chrome, logo, or added copy.

## Motion and timing

- Portrait fades in first, divider draws left to right, then year and headline rise/fade in.
- Hold on the completed composition. 180 frames at 30 fps.

## Layers

- Background and divider are native ChrononTemplate shapes.
- Portrait is the user-provided crop, preserved as an image layer.
- Year and headline are editable text layers.

## Preview acceptance

- Preview matches the reference hierarchy and tone, keeps all text readable, and shows the portrait crop without YouTube controls or subtitles.
- Verify opening, middle, and ending frames before delivery.
