# Recreated Web Phrase Scenes — Contract

## Source and scope

Rebuild the four supplied web-page phrase references as native Chronon render plans. The reference screenshots provide wording, approximate typography, and composition only. Do not use any full screenshot as a background plate. The scene named `web_phrase_kaycee_question` recreates the clipped headline “Is it possible that Kaycee did not exist”; the other three scenes recreate the $450 excerpt and the two views of the MMS passage. `web_phrase_mms_double_highlight_camera` reuses the two MMS highlights as a sequential camera move.

## Composition

- Canvas: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 210 frames (7 seconds), software render backend.
- Background: warm, near-white page field; text is charcoal for selected text and subdued gray for surrounding article copy.
- Phrase marker: saturated yellow, rectangular, drawn left to right beneath/behind the selected phrase.
- Kaycee question: a single bold phrase centered horizontally, with broad negative space around it.
- MMS views: editable article text centered in the canvas, with the phrases “sold tens of thousands of bottles of MMS” and “Weekly Testimonies” highlighted. Full-page view uses smaller text than the zoom view.
- Price view: two lines of article copy with “$450” highlighted.
- Double-highlight sequence: the full passage and both yellow markers finish appearing before the camera push. The camera centers on “sold tens of thousands of bottles of MMS,” holds, then glides to “Weekly Testimonies” and holds through the ending.

## Motion and layers

- Paper background is a native color layer.
- Every text run and each marker is a separate editable Chronon layer.
- Text fades and rises slightly into place. Yellow markers draw on horizontally before the phrase finishes settling.
- No YouTube controls, captions, browser chrome, screenshot borders, divider timeline, photographic insert, or extra headline may appear in these four web phrase scenes.

## Approval and review

Before export, review a representative animated frame from each plan at 1920×1080 for alignment, legibility, and marker placement. After export, inspect opening, middle, and ending frames for every clip. Deliver the MP4s, representative stills, and a contact sheet under `out/web_long_short_v2/recreated_phrases/`.
