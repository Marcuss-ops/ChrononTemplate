# NBA Fast Intro Board — 10 Players

## Scene contract

- **Subject and assets:** Ten well-known Celtics–Heat rivalry players from the existing, attributed NBA asset pack: Paul Pierce, LeBron James, Kevin Garnett, Dwyane Wade, Ray Allen, Chris Bosh, Rajon Rondo, Udonis Haslem, Kendrick Perkins, and Shane Battier. Reuse the prepared portrait cards and corkboard texture.
- **Composition and camera:** Warm corkboard fills a 1920×1080 frame. All ten portrait cards sit together in a loose five-by-two arrangement with varied small rotations. Each card has its own yellow paper note, overlapping one corner, with its `#1`–`#10` number in red. No extra title or interface.
- **Motion and timing:** 30 seconds at 30 fps. Hold on the whole board first. Then make a slow, gentle camera move to each card in order, push closer, hold, and continue to the next. End on the tenth card.
- **Layers and source:** Chronon layers keep the corkboard, each portrait card, and each numbered-note artwork separate. Each yellow note and red number are a single transparent-free PNG artwork; the camera position and orientation are animated in the render plan.
- **Must not appear:** More or fewer than ten player cards, duplicate cards, blue pins, unrelated captions, browser UI, or a flattened screenshot of the full composition.
- **Preview acceptance:** Inspect the opening overview and representative camera stops for the first, middle, and last cards. Every card must be visible in the overview; each number must stay attached to its note; each close-up must keep the numbered player card in frame. Inspect decoded opening, middle, and ending frames before delivery.
