# Trump Style portrait source

`donald_trump_oval_office_2025.jpg` is an official White House photograph of President Donald Trump signing executive orders in the Oval Office, February 2025. Source gallery: https://www.whitehouse.gov/gallery/president-trump-signs-executive-orders-in-the-oval-office/ . The image is credited on the gallery page to the Official White House Photo Office. This local copy is used as the portrait layer in `tools/entities_with_text/trump_entity_editorial_v1.py`.
