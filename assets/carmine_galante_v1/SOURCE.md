# Carmine Galante archival portrait

`carmine_galante_1947.jpg` is the publicly available 1947 NYPD mugshot of Carmine Galante, credited to the New York Police Department. Wikimedia Commons marks the image public domain and records the source/license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carmine_Galante_1947.jpg . `carmine_galante_1947_print.png` is a warm monochrome print treatment made from that source. `galante_caseboard.png` is a locally generated background plate. The scene and motion are authored by `tools/entities_with_text/carmine_galante_archive_v1.py`.
